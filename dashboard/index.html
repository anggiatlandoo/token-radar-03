Graveyard Radar

Every other radar ranks the protocols that might launch. This one starts with the ones that almost certainly will not — and counts what you save by walking past them.

The Graveyard The Board The Model

P(launch) is forward-looking: it is the share of a protocol’s cohort that launches after its current age, divided by the share still pre-token at that age, plus a 5% late-launch tail after year 3 — so an old protocol that has not launched scores as unlikely, not likely. The launch table itself is the cumulative share already tokenized, calibrated by a time split. A protocol on this page records no token; that is not proof it has none.
